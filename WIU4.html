About the game

Detective Dexter and Vector's Vanquishing is the third chapter of the series, transitioning into a fully 3D bullet heaven arena built in Unity during Year 3 Semester 2. After two seasons of fighting Vector's army, Detective Dexter faces down the last of the robot legion and defeats Vector once and for all. The theme is Boss Fight: players must survive enemy hordes, build up synergistic loadouts, and prepare their stats before activating the boss portal to face a significantly more powerful final boss.

Attacks are automatic, keeping the focus on movement, loadout decisions, and stat upgrades. The development cycle emphasised modular, decoupled architecture to allow rapid weapon tuning and clean stat progression without disrupting existing core loops.

What I built

Built the full player controller with a smooth state machine covering Idle, Run, Jump, Slide, Fall, and Death states, plus custom crowd-pushing physics to let the player move freely through large enemy swarms.
Engineered a data-driven stat engine tracking 20+ upgradeable stats including Damage, Speed, Luck, Block, and Thorns, with real-time XP levelling, stat growth curves, and automated Health and Shield regeneration.
Implemented the loot pickup system with object pooling for hundreds of simultaneous XP orb and coin drops, magnet effects, and pickup ranges that scale dynamically with player stats.
Designed a Passive Item and Rarity system where stackable buffs scale in power based on their rolled rarity tier, backed by a randomiser used across chests, level-up rewards, and the shop.
Programmed the Level Up logic, Reward Randomiser, and Merchant logic handling price randomisation, gold spending, and inventory updates for the shop NPC.
Engineered a cinematic camera system with a smooth orbital main camera with dynamic zoom, and a dedicated framing camera that focuses on the player and final boss at spawn.
Scripted all dynamic world interactables: Challenge Shrines that spawn enemy waves with a remaining count display, Zone Shrines for area-capture events, the Boss Portal that summons the final boss and updates objectives, and both dropped and paid loot chests.
Built the text popup manager for damage numbers and floating text, an item randomiser for chests, shops, and level-up panels, and a random spawn system that places interactables around the map with ground snapping.

Outcome

Successfully delivered an optimized stat management framework capable of driving immediate upgrade reactions across extensive combat properties. The separate architecture wrapper isolated gameplay statistics from base rendering loops, allowing rapid iteration of weapon parameters and loadout synergies.
