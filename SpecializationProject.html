About the game

Card Crawler fuses high-octane shooter combat with the dense horde-based survival mechanics of a bullet heaven FPS. You play as a professional card thrower trapped in a magical dungeon where your cards act as your sole survival tool. Players explore individual rooms and fight waves of enemies to progress toward a boss guarding the exit.

The gameplay centers on tactical synergies and a dynamic deck-building system. Every card thrown carries its rarity as a throw multiplier, allowing effects to stack additively across subsequent throws and trigger massive chain reactions. This build establishes the core card engine, combat systems, and room logic as a foundation for the full dungeon crawler experience.

What I built so far

Designed and programmed a first-person player controller with movement, jump, dash, and double jump mechanics.
Implemented first-person combat with card-throwing projectiles and close-quarters melee punch.
Architected a multi-layered Card Engine handling deck logic, hand management, and a rarity-based carryover system where card rarity acts as a throw multiplier, allowing effects to stack additively.
Engineered stackable status debuffs: stacking Bleed that combines damage into one effect, duration-refreshing Poison, and multi-instance Burn that applies separate damage ticks.
Programmed basic projectile card effects including Critical damage multipliers, target-penetrating Piercing, Homing vectors, and projectile size modifiers.
Built buff card effects including on-hit Explosive triggers, Lifesteal recovery, Multishot projectile counts, Rapid Fire throw rate increases, Draw Card on throw, and Repeater repeat attacks.
Developed room logic with automated door containment and wave-based enemy spawning, plus a simplified room generation system that chains rooms in sequence.
Built UI covering player health bars, enemy counters, active effect tracking, and a post-room card reward selection panel with three random card options.
Implemented main menu, pause menu, loading screen, and a basic death screen.
